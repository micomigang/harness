1. Fully stop the old Harness process and restart after overwrite.
2. Do NOT rerun source analysis, screenplay, or Asset Manifest rev5.
3. Open the current Characters node (rev1).
4. Regenerate Characters once with a narrow instruction:
   - keep the same 5 canonical character identities, plot, relationships, performance and voice anchors;
   - separate source_visual_traits from localized_visual_design;
   - culturally adapt source-specific wardrobe to France;
   - keep narrative function, age/class contrast, recognisable colour family and story-critical accessories;
   - generation_prompt_en must be English visual instructions;
   - do not generate images during this text-stage regeneration.
5. Expect Characters rev2. Inspect char_grandmere first:
   - source_visual_traits may mention the source red/floral/padded rural coat;
   - localized_visual_design / wardrobe should be a plausible French-countryside equivalent, not a literal Chinese-style large red floral padded jacket;
   - generation_prompt_en should be English.
6. In the visual candidate workbench, generate only 1 candidate for char_grandmere first.
7. If unsatisfied, enter a local adjustment such as:
   "Keep the same woman and burgundy identity colour; make the clothing more understated contemporary French countryside, less source-culture floral padded styling."
   Then generate again based on the current candidate.
8. When satisfied, click "设为采用并锁定". That selected image becomes the visual anchor used by the Reference Images stage.
9. Proceed to Scenes and Props normally. New scene/prop generations automatically use the same source-vs-localized-design contract.

Fallback behavior:
- If you do not regenerate Characters, the existing rev1 can still generate candidates: Seedream receives the new France visual-localization prompt compiler. However the old character-bible text will still show the old wardrobe wording, so regenerating once is cleaner and auditable.
